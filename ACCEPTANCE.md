# Glove acceptance record

Each first-release clause in [CONTRACT.md](CONTRACT.md) (`GLOVE-01` to `GLOVE-12` and `HTTP-001`) is proven by one flow through Glove's public doors, plus real proof for the high-stakes paths (authority, destructive move-in undo, export and restore). Status **LOCAL PROVEN** means the named test passed in `rer verify` from a fresh clone of the recorded commit; it does not claim hosted operation, provider delivery, a reconciled real ServiceM8 export, or a buyer's acceptance.
