Status **LOCAL PROVEN** means the named test passed in `rer verify` from a fresh clone of the recorded commit; it does not claim hosted operation, provider delivery, a reconciled real ServiceM8 export, or a buyer's acceptance.

## Boundaries

- The ServiceM8 kit is proven against `tests/fixtures/servicem8-small-hvac`, authored from ServiceM8's documented API field names. No real owner export has been reconciled; that is the next acceptance step with the first buyer.
- The invoice CSV follows Xero's documented sales-invoice import columns; it has not been imported into a live Xero organisation.
- Phone capture is proven in headless Chromium with the network switched off; no physical device or long offline period was tested. Photos are kept in the browser's local storage while waiting, which is limited (the screen says so when full).
- No hosted deployment, SMS, email delivery, maps, payments or accounting integration is claimed.
