Technicians use the same Glove on their phone. Signed in with the Technician role, they see **Your visits**: only the visits assigned to them, with no prices.

## On a visit

Open a visit to see the address, time, customer and the site's access notes. Then:

- **Record arrival** when you get there. Glove records the phone's time.
- **Checklist**: tick each item done, or mark it **N/A** with a reason.
- **Notes**: anything about the job or access.
- **Photos**: taken with the camera and shrunk on the phone to at most 1600 px before they are sent.
- **Materials used**: from the price book, or described, with a quantity.

## When the signal drops

Every item goes into the phone's outbox first and shows **Waiting to send** until Glove has it. With no signal the screen says, for example, **Offline — 5 items waiting to send.** They are kept on this phone and send when you reconnect; **Send now** sends them straight away once you are back online. An item that was already received is never recorded twice.

Keep using the same phone and browser until everything has sent: the items are stored in that browser.

## Complete the visit

**Complete visit** needs a recorded arrival. Write what was done, then either the customer signs on the screen with their name, or you say why they could not sign. Leave **This finishes the job** ticked unless a return visit is needed. A job completes only when every required checklist item is done or marked not applicable with a reason.

The completed job is then ready for an invoice; see [Invoices and exports](/docs/invoices).
