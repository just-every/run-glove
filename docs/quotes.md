Quotes live under **Quotes**: enquiries, priced quotes and the customer's answer.

## Record an enquiry

Choose **Record an enquiry**: the customer, the site, how it came in and what the customer needs, then **Record enquiry**. Open enquiries are listed with **Quote it**. An enquiry you will not quote can be closed with a reason.

## Price a quote

A quote is a list of priced lines. Pick services from your price book or write a custom line with its quantity and unit price ex GST. Glove adds GST at 10% of the taxable subtotal, rounded half up once, so the total is always to the cent. A line can be GST-free.

## Approve the price

A person approves one exact revision's price: **Approve revision 1 at $…**. An agent can draft a quote but never approve it. Changing any price after approval is a new revision that needs its own approval; earlier revisions and approvals stay on the record.

## Record how it went out

Once approved, **Record how you sent it** (email, printed, in person or phone) and **Record as sent**. Glove records what a person did; it sends nothing itself and claims no delivery.

## Record the customer's answer

When the customer answers, record **acceptance** or **decline** of the current revision: their name, how they answered (signed, email reply, phone, in person) and any note. An accepted quote is frozen. It then waits on the dispatch board under **Waiting to book** until someone creates its job; see [Dispatch and visits](/docs/dispatch).
